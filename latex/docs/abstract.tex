\begin{abstract}
无人机（UAV）集群为动态地面需求提供时限计算服务时，需要协调空间覆盖、跨节点分工与有限传输计算资源。轨迹策略若看不到尚未接纳的需求，难以及时形成服务机会；增加接纳又可能加剧批量资源竞争与跨时隙排队。本文以临时空地计算服务为应用动机，围绕需求感知轨迹、固定快照任务分配和动态排队联合控制开展研究。以全部实际生成请求为分母定义端到端截止期满足率（DSR），并通过接纳率与接纳后完成率分解其变化。

首先，在原含能量与缓存的自然覆盖协议下，保持 $100\,\mathrm{m}$ 物理服务半径不变，将感知范围内尚未覆盖需求纳入 Attention-MAPPO 的预动作观测。独立确认中，扩展观测候选 DSR 从0.2444增至0.2793，说明需求可见性能够改善指定主场景的接纳与服务。其次，针对跨机任务迁移通过最终批量共享算力影响其他请求的现象，提出整体重评估的 MC-ITR 固定快照求解方法。主配置平均超时数由迭代对照的2.228降至2.055；历史共享轨迹快照进一步显示分配质量余量，同时伴随较高分配能耗与搜索开销。MC-ITR 未作为后续动态排队方法的在线模块或蒸馏教师。

进一步，在独立的无能量动态排队协议中，研究上传、转发和 EDF 计算跨时隙保留进度时的轨迹与卸载联合控制。构建候选级双分支策略，通过两轮数据聚合蒸馏规划知识，再利用完整环境轨迹与共同随机数下的短视界反事实辅助评分进行 PPO 微调。该辅助评分与真实轨迹奖励分别定义，不将混合更新视为标准在策略 GRPO。测试提供零在线 Engine 前瞻策略，以及学习先验同时参与候选筛选和评分的 Top-6 MPC。

三个固定父谱系、八个新工作负载与两个动作流构成816个配对确认回合。联合微调 DSR 为48.74\%，相对蒸馏学生初值48.04\%提高0.70个百分点，31项比较校正区间为 $[+0.39,+0.98]$ 个百分点。微调 Top-6 MPC 为49.31\%，相对指定在线规划基线48.56\%提高0.75个百分点，校正区间为 $[+0.25,+1.42]$ 个百分点。两种控制方式平均决策耗时分别为0.520秒和0.722秒，低于基线1.602秒，但尾延迟仍超过一秒；耦合结构及反事实项的独立收益尚未分离确证。第五章未建模推进能耗、通信能耗、UE 无线供能与电池动态，其绝对指标不能与第三、四章直接拼接。本文据此形成从需求可见性、批量资源竞争到动态排队控制的分层证据，为进一步纳入信息成本、决策延迟及能量约束的集群服务方法提供基础。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
A UAV swarm providing deadline-constrained computation services must coordinate spatial coverage, task offloading, and limited communication and computing resources. A trajectory policy that cannot observe unadmitted demand may miss service opportunities, whereas admitting more requests can increase batch contention and persistent queues. Motivated by temporary air--ground computation services, this thesis studies demand-aware trajectories, fixed-snapshot allocation, and joint control under dynamic queues. End-to-end deadline satisfaction rate (DSR) uses all actually generated requests as its denominator and is decomposed into admission and conditional completion.

First, under the original natural-coverage protocol with energy and caching dynamics, uncovered demand within the sensing range is included in the pre-action observations of Attention-MAPPO while retaining the 100-m service radius. Independent confirmation increases DSR from 0.2444 to 0.2793 in the specified main setting. Second, the fixed-snapshot MC-ITR method reevaluates complete assignments to account for the effects of task migration on other requests through batch-shared computing. It reduces mean deadline misses from 2.228 for an iterative comparator to 2.055. Historical shared-trajectory snapshots identify further allocation headroom, accompanied by higher assignment energy and search cost. MC-ITR is neither an online component nor the distillation teacher of the subsequent dynamic-queue method.

An independent energy-free protocol is then used to study joint trajectory and offloading control when upload, forwarding, and EDF computing retain progress across slots. A two-branch candidate policy first acquires planning knowledge through two rounds of data aggregation and distillation. Complete environment trajectories and short-horizon counterfactual scores under common random numbers then support PPO fine-tuning. Counterfactual scores are distinguished from actual trajectory rewards, and the mixed update is not interpreted as standard on-policy GRPO. Evaluation considers a policy without online Engine lookahead and a neural-guided Top-6 MPC in which the learned prior affects both candidate selection and scoring.

Confirmation comprises 816 paired evaluation episodes across three fixed parent lineages, eight new workloads, and two action streams. Joint fine-tuning achieves 48.74\% DSR, improving on its distilled student initialization of 48.04\% by 0.70 percentage points; the interval corrected across 31 comparisons is [+0.39, +0.98] percentage points. Fine-tuned Top-6 MPC achieves 49.31\%, exceeding the specified online planner's 48.56\% by 0.75 percentage points, with a corrected interval of [+0.25, +1.42]. Mean decision times are 0.520 and 0.722 seconds, compared with 1.602 seconds for the planner, although tail latencies still exceed one second. Independent gains from coupling and the counterfactual term are not established. The dynamic-queue protocol excludes propulsion energy, communication energy, wireless power transfer, and battery dynamics, so its absolute results cannot be pooled with those of Chapters 3 and 4. These findings provide bounded evidence connecting demand visibility, batch contention, and dynamic-queue control, and a basis for studying reporting costs, decision delays, and energy constraints.
\end{abstract*}
