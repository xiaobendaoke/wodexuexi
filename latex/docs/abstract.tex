\begin{abstract}
随着低空经济与智能应急响应的快速发展，无人机集群辅助移动边缘计算已成为向地面动态终端提供低时延计算服务的重要形态。在实际空地协同服务过程中，受限于机载物理覆盖半径、异构服务缓存与计算容量以及多节点资源竞争，无人机轨迹机动、前端任务接纳与多节点卸载分配之间呈现出强烈的时空耦合特征。围绕受限自然覆盖与多节点资源竞争下如何提升系统端到端截止期满足率这一核心问题，本文依次对空地协同计算统一建模、需求感知协同轨迹规划、二元字典序任务分配以及跨时隙动态排队协同控制展开系统研究。主要工作如下：

针对空地协同计算系统中空间几何关联、多链路无线传输与异构节点负载紧密耦合的问题，面向时限计算服务需求，构建了空地协同计算统一数学模型与双协议评价体系。该模型采用高低层控制时间尺度分离架构，建立了最近邻自然覆盖关联准则、离散机动安全间距修复算子与频段隔离视距通信模型，并严格证明最小安全间距约束下各机自然覆盖关联集合具备互斥完备划分性质。在此基础上，统一刻画本地计算、邻机协同中继与地面宏基站回传三类执行路径，分别针对单时隙批量等份算力共享与跨时隙先占式最早截止期优先串联排队建立两类互补负载模型。同时，给出端到端截止期满足率的双因子精确归因分解与同源工作负载配对评价准则，实现了前端空间接纳能力与后端资源执行质量的解耦量化，为后续协同规划与控制方法设计奠定了理论与评价基础。

在单时隙自然覆盖与批量算力共享场景下，受限覆盖半径使圈外终端在局部观测中不可见，易引发上层策略观测混叠。为此，在分层多智能体强化学习框架下将感知范围内的未覆盖需求融入上层表征并与精简下层策略协同寻优，提出需求感知协同轨迹规划方法。理论分析从策略梯度可观测性出发，证明了仅含已关联终端的局部观测对圈外需求分布存在梯度盲区；系统瓶颈诊断亦表明，未覆盖丢弃占全部失败请求的 $68.55\%$，是制约时限服务性能的主导因素。所提方法在不扩大物理覆盖半径且不增加固定观测张量维度的前提下，按距离升序将感知范围内的未覆盖活跃终端填入上层关系自注意力网络的空闲槽位，并联合下层无注意力精简卸载策略开展跨层协同训练。仿真结果表明，在跨多组独立训练种子与未知工作负载的测试场景下，面对累计生成请求达 $9.81\times 10^6$ 个的独立测试集，该方法将端到端截止期满足率由 $24.50\%$ 提升至 $27.35\%$，净增 $2.85$ 个百分点。

针对单时隙批量等份算力共享下单个请求跨机迁移会改变源节点与目标节点同批其余任务完成时延的耦合机理，依托全批次重评估机制替代个体局部代价估计，提出多轮协同迭代任务分配算法（MC-ITR）。该算法构建了严格二元字典序优化目标，依次将超时请求总数最小化与平均归一化时延最小化设为第一、第二优先级，克服了传统线性加权将异质指标折合的局限。围绕任务迁移的全局影响，定量推导出单请求跨机迁移在源节点算力释放与目标节点算力摊薄两侧产生的时延外部性增量。理论分析严格证明，算法在全批次重评估准则下具备单步严格字典序下降性、有限步终止性与单请求邻域局部最优性。在 $410$ 个主配置评估单元与 $400$ 个高负载轨迹采样快照上的配对实验表明，MC-ITR 将每快照平均超时请求数分别由启发式基线的 $2.888$ 降至 $2.045$、由在线下层卸载策略的 $9.238$ 降至 $6.605$，较自利多轮迭代分别净减 $0.170$ 个与 $0.873$ 个。

面向突发热点需求到达、已接纳关联不可变与历史队列积压持久耦合的跨时隙控制难题，将离线多步规划知识蒸馏、共同随机数反事实微调与先验裁剪模型预测控制有机结合，设计了跨时隙动态排队联合控制框架。为刻画跨时隙积压演化，耦合单通道通信释放时刻递推与节点工作守恒先占式最早截止期优先调度，建立了两阶段串联排队近似模型。针对多候选协同决策，构建了融入上层期望运动反馈的双分支策略网络，并严格证明其候选槽位置换同变性。训练阶段采用两轮在线状态聚合将离线多步规划教师偏好蒸馏至策略网络，再引入共同随机数三步反事实辅助优势项开展近端策略优化微调。理论分析证明反事实组中心化可消去公共到达波动干扰，使微调策略具备无需在线前瞻仿真的快速推理能力。此外，结合熵自适应对数先验权重校准，构建了先验裁剪的神经引导滚动规划方案。在包含 $252{,}024$ 个累计生成请求的 $816$ 个跨时隙测试回合上的配对仿真表明，反事实辅助微调策略将端到端截止期满足率由初始先验的 $44.30\%$ 提升至 $48.74\%$，单步决策耗时较在线规划缩短 $67.5\%$ 至 $0.520\,\mathrm{s}$；结合温度校准 Top-$6$ 滚动验证后，满足率进一步提升至 $49.31\%$，单步决策耗时较在线规划缩短 $54.9\%$ 至 $0.722\,\mathrm{s}$。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
With the rapid development of the low-altitude economy and intelligent emergency response, unmanned aerial vehicle swarm-assisted mobile edge computing has become an important paradigm for providing low-latency computation services to dynamic ground terminals. In practical air-ground collaborative operations, limited onboard physical coverage radius, heterogeneous service caching and computing capacities, and multi-node resource contention induce strong spatiotemporal coupling among trajectory maneuvering, front-end task admission, and multi-node offloading allocation. Focusing on improving the system end-to-end deadline satisfaction rate under constrained natural coverage and multi-node resource contention, this thesis systematically investigates unified air-ground collaborative computing modeling, demand-aware cooperative trajectory planning, binary lexicographic task allocation, and cross-slot dynamic queuing control. The main work is summarized as follows:

Addressing the tight coupling among spatial geometric association, multi-link wireless transmission, and heterogeneous node workloads, this thesis establishes a unified air-ground collaborative computing model and a dual-protocol evaluation framework for deadline-constrained services. Under a two-timescale control architecture, nearest-neighbor natural-coverage association rules, collision-avoidance safety-spacing repair operators, and frequency-isolated line-of-sight wireless channel models are formulated, and the mutually exclusive and exhaustive partition property of natural-coverage association sets under minimum safety spacing is rigorously proved. Building on this foundation, local onboard computing, neighbor-relay collaboration, and macro base station backhaul execution paths are uniformly characterized, with two complementary workload models established for single-slot batch equal-share computing and cross-slot preemptive earliest-deadline-first tandem queuing, respectively. Furthermore, an exact two-factor attribution decomposition of the end-to-end deadline satisfaction rate and a common-workload paired evaluation criterion are derived, decoupling front-end spatial admission capability from back-end resource execution quality and laying the theoretical and evaluation foundation for subsequent planning and control designs.

Under single-slot natural coverage and batch equal-share computing, restricted physical coverage renders out-of-coverage terminals invisible in local observations, causing upper-level observation aliasing. By incorporating uncovered demand within the sensing range into upper-level representations and optimizing them jointly with a streamlined lower-level policy under hierarchical multi-agent reinforcement learning, a demand-aware cooperative trajectory planning method is proposed. From the perspective of policy-gradient observability, associated-only local observations are proved to suffer from a gradient blind spot with respect to out-of-coverage demand distributions, while bottleneck diagnostics reveal that uncovered drops account for $68.55\%$ of all failed requests and constitute the primary factor limiting deadline-constrained service performance. Without expanding the physical coverage radius or increasing fixed observation tensor dimensions, the proposed method populates vacant slots of the upper-level relational self-attention network with uncovered active terminals within the sensing range in ascending order of distance, and trains it cooperatively with an attention-free streamlined lower-level offloading policy. Paired simulations across test scenarios spanning multiple independent training seeds and unseen workloads on an independent test set of $9.81\times 10^6$ cumulatively generated requests demonstrate that the proposed method increases the end-to-end deadline satisfaction rate from $24.50\%$ to $27.35\%$, achieving a net gain of $2.85$ percentage points.

Addressing the coupling mechanism whereby migrating a single request across nodes under single-slot batch equal-share computing alters completion latencies of co-scheduled tasks on both source and destination nodes, full-batch re-evaluation is introduced to replace local individual cost estimation, leading to a multi-round cooperative iterative task allocation algorithm (MC-ITR). The algorithm formulates a strict binary lexicographic objective that prioritizes minimizing total deadline misses followed by mean normalized latency, overcoming the limitation of conventional linear scalarization in conflating heterogeneous metrics. Regarding global migration effects, exact latency externality increments resulting from computing capacity release at the source node and capacity dilution at the destination node are derived. Theoretical analysis rigorously proves that MC-ITR guarantees single-step strict lexicographic descent, finite-step termination, and single-request neighborhood local optimality under full-batch re-evaluation. Paired experiments across $410$ main evaluation units and $400$ high-load trajectory-sampled snapshots show that MC-ITR reduces average deadline misses per snapshot from $2.888$ for the heuristic baseline to $2.045$, and from $9.238$ for the online lower-level offloading policy to $6.605$, achieving net reductions of $0.170$ and $0.873$ misses beyond self-interested multi-round iteration, respectively.

To address cross-slot control under coupled bursty hotspot arrivals, immutable post-admission associations, and persistent queue backlogs, a cross-slot dynamic queuing joint control framework is designed by integrating offline multi-step planning distillation, common random number counterfactual fine-tuning, and prior-pruned model predictive control. To capture cross-slot backlog evolution, a two-stage tandem queuing approximation model is established by coupling single-channel communication release-time recursion with work-conserving preemptive earliest-deadline-first node scheduling. For multi-candidate cooperative decision-making, a dual-branch policy network incorporating upper-level expected motion feedback is constructed, with its candidate-slot permutation equivariance rigorously proved. During policy learning, two-round online state aggregation distills offline multi-step planning preferences into the policy network, followed by proximal policy optimization fine-tuning with a three-step common random number counterfactual auxiliary advantage. Theoretical analysis proves that counterfactual group centering cancels common arrival disturbances, enabling fast inference without online lookahead simulation. Combined with entropy-adaptive log-prior weight calibration, a prior-pruned neural-guided receding-horizon planning scheme is further constructed. Paired simulations across $816$ cross-slot test episodes comprising $252{,}024$ cumulatively generated requests show that the counterfactual-assisted fine-tuned policy raises the end-to-end deadline satisfaction rate from $44.30\%$ for the initial prior to $48.74\%$ while cutting per-slot decision time by $67.5\%$ compared with online planning to $0.520\,\mathrm{s}$; incorporating temperature-calibrated Top-$6$ receding-horizon verification further increases the satisfaction rate to $49.31\%$ while cutting per-slot decision time by $54.9\%$ compared with online planning to $0.722\,\mathrm{s}$.
\end{abstract*}
