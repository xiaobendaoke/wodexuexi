\begin{abstract}
多无人机辅助移动边缘计算系统需要同时决定无人机轨迹与服务请求的执行位置。在自然覆盖接纳规则下，未进入无人机服务半径的请求不能通过卸载算法补救；已经接纳的请求又可能因计算与通信资源竞争而超时。本文围绕这两个环节建立分层决策与评价框架：研究上层轨迹策略对未覆盖需求的观测，在固定轨迹快照上研究请求级卸载搜索，并评估完整学习式分层方法的端到端服务表现。完整方法采用 Attention-MAPPO 上层与无注意力的学习式卸载下层；固定快照搜索方法 MC-ITR 单独评估，并未在当前系统中直接集成。

在五架无人机、一百个用户设备的仿真主场景中，完整候选方法将上层预动作观测扩展至感知范围内尚未接纳的用户。十个训练种子与五个配对工作负载的整套方法对比表明，候选方法的截止期满足率（DSR）为 $0.2798$，最强的已有学习式双层对照为 $0.2459$；差值为 $3.39$ 个百分点，训练种子配对 $95\%$ 置信区间为 $[2.13,4.66]$ 个百分点。相对预先指定的五个学习式对手，DSR 差值均为正且通过 Holm 校正后的双侧检验。固定轨迹快照实验中，MC-ITR 每快照平均超时数为 $2.055$，低于迭代对照的 $2.228$，但能耗和搜索耗时更高。

在完整方法的轨迹快照上，MC-ITR 比在线下层平均每快照少 $2.460$ 个超时请求，但分配能耗约高 $23.8\%$，完整调用路径约需 $20.751\,\mathrm{s}$；该反事实比较不代表长期在线 DSR。上述结果支持候选整套方法在所评估仿真主场景和现有方法原配置下的 DSR 优势；固定潜在服务请求分母的敏感性分析保持了这一结论。现有证据不支持将增益单独归因于注意力架构，也不代表所有指标均优。预接纳信息的获取成本、同信息预算比较和跨负载稳健性仍待验证。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
A multi-UAV mobile edge computing system must coordinate trajectory control with per-request task offloading. Under natural coverage-based admission, an offloading decision cannot recover a service request that lies outside every UAV's coverage area; admitted requests may still miss their deadlines because of resource competition. This thesis examines upper-layer observation of uncovered demand, request-level offloading on fixed trajectory snapshots, and the end-to-end performance of a hierarchical learning-based system. The full system uses an Attention-MAPPO upper layer and a learned lower layer without attention. The fixed-snapshot MC-ITR solver is evaluated separately and is not used as the online lower layer.

In the simulated main setting with five UAVs and 100 user equipments, the candidate system expands the upper-layer pre-action observation to include unadmitted users within the sensing range. A whole-method comparison with ten training seeds and five paired workload seeds yields a deadline satisfaction rate (DSR) of 0.2798 for the candidate and 0.2459 for the strongest existing learned hierarchical comparator. The paired difference is 3.39 percentage points, with a training-seed-level 95\% confidence interval of [2.13, 4.66] percentage points. DSR differences against all five prespecified learned comparators are positive and pass Holm-adjusted two-sided tests. In fixed-snapshot experiments, MC-ITR reduces the mean number of deadline misses per snapshot from 2.228 for an iterative comparator to 2.055, at higher assignment-energy and search-time costs.

On snapshots from the candidate policy, MC-ITR produced 2.460 fewer deadline misses per snapshot than the online lower layer on average, with approximately 23.8\% higher assignment energy and a 20.751-s full-path call time. This counterfactual does not establish an improvement in long-run online DSR. The results support a DSR advantage for the complete candidate system in the evaluated simulated main setting under each method's original configuration; a sensitivity analysis using a common latent-service denominator preserves this finding. The evidence does not isolate an attention-architecture effect or establish superiority on every metric. Equal-information comparisons, pre-admission reporting costs, and cross-load robustness remain to be tested.
\end{abstract*}
