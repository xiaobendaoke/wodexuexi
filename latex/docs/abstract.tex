\begin{abstract}
随着 5G 技术的快速发展和迭代，多无人机辅助移动边缘计算（Multi-UAV MEC）系统逐渐成为未来通信网络的重要组成部分。凭借灵活的机动部署、视距链路和按需覆盖等优势，无人机辅助边缘计算在应急通信、智慧城市、低空智能网络等场景中展现出巨大的应用潜力，可有效增强边缘覆盖、任务接入和协同计算能力。然而，多无人机 MEC 系统的优化仍然面临诸多挑战，包括无人机轨迹规划、请求级卸载决策、截止期约束、宏基站（MBS）回传负载以及终端设备能量动态等问题。由于无人机的飞行特性和无线信道环境的动态变化，传统的启发式优化算法难以高效应对这些复杂问题。近年来，多智能体强化学习（Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, MARL）算法在解决复杂的协同决策问题中表现出色。因此，将 MARL 算法应用于多无人机 MEC 系统的轨迹与任务卸载协同优化研究，以提升系统服务质量、降低 UAV 侧能耗、改善用户公平性，具有重要的研究意义。

本文面向多无人机 MEC 系统中轨迹控制与任务卸载的强耦合问题，提出基于多智能体强化学习的双层协同优化框架。系统中，服务请求优先关联至自然覆盖范围内的 UAV；对于未被自然覆盖的请求，按照最近 UAV 原则完成关联，并在真实链路条件下进入后续处理流程。具体研究内容与贡献如下：

（1）针对连续轨迹控制与离散卸载决策联合学习导致训练复杂度高的问题，本文提出基于注意力机制的多智能体近端策略优化（Attention-MAPPO）双层框架。该框架将协同调度分解为上层轨迹控制和下层请求级卸载两个决策层：上层利用 Attention-MAPPO 建模无人机间协同关系并输出轨迹动作；下层利用约束注意力卸载 MAPPO，为每个有效服务请求选择本地 UAV、协作 UAV 或 MBS 执行。仿真结果表明，所提框架能够稳定收敛，并提升系统综合奖励。

（2）针对多 UAV 协同场景下不可行动作探索效率低、约束权衡难以刻画的问题，本文设计请求级质量感知动作掩码和拉格朗日约束机制。动作掩码在运行时屏蔽明显不可行的协作 UAV 与 MBS 动作，减少无效探索；拉格朗日约束将截止时间满足率（deadline satisfaction rate, DSR）和 MBS 负载纳入可解释的软约束权衡。仿真结果表明，所提机制能够调节 MBS 依赖和协作卸载比例，并刻画 DSR、能耗与 MBS 负载之间的偏好关系。

（3）针对现有工作忽略终端设备能量动态、难以反映系统长期运行特性的问题，本文显式建模 UAV 飞行能耗、用户设备（user equipment, UE）电池动态和无线能量传输（wireless power transfer, WPT）机制。对比实验和参数敏感性实验表明，在统一的服务请求关联与处理流程下，所提框架取得最高服务公平性、最低 UAV 侧能耗和最低 MBS 负载比例，同时保持具有竞争力的 DSR；在不同 UE 负载和 UAV 算力条件下，该框架仍表现出稳定的能效、公平性与服务质量折中能力。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
With the rapid development and iteration of 5G technology, multi-UAV assisted mobile edge computing (Multi-UAV MEC) systems have gradually become an important component of future communication networks. With the advantages of flexible maneuvering deployment, line-of-sight links, and on-demand coverage, UAV-assisted edge computing has shown great application potential in scenarios such as emergency communications, smart cities, and low-altitude intelligent networks, which can effectively enhance edge coverage, task admission, and cooperative computing capabilities. However, the optimization of multi-UAV MEC systems still faces numerous challenges, including UAV trajectory planning, per-request offloading decisions, deadline constraints, macro base station (MBS) backhaul load, and terminal device energy dynamics. Due to the flight characteristics of UAVs and the dynamic changes of wireless channel environments, traditional heuristic optimization algorithms are difficult to efficiently address these complex problems. In recent years, multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) algorithms have shown excellent performance in solving complex collaborative decision-making problems. Therefore, applying MARL algorithms to the collaborative optimization of trajectory and task offloading in multi-UAV MEC systems, in order to improve system service quality, reduce UAV-side energy consumption, and enhance user fairness, has important research significance.

This paper proposes a hierarchical multi-agent reinforcement learning framework to address the strong coupling between trajectory control and task offloading in multi-UAV MEC systems. Service requests are first associated with UAVs that naturally cover them; requests outside natural coverage are associated with the nearest UAV and subsequently processed under the corresponding physical link conditions. The main contributions are as follows:

(1) To reduce the training complexity of jointly learning continuous trajectory control and discrete offloading decisions, this paper proposes an attention-based multi-agent proximal policy optimization (Attention-MAPPO) framework. The framework decomposes collaborative scheduling into an upper trajectory-control layer and a lower per-request offloading layer. The upper layer uses Attention-MAPPO to model inter-UAV coordination and generate trajectory actions, whereas the lower layer uses constrained attention offload MAPPO to select local UAV execution, cooperative UAV execution, or MBS execution for each valid service request. Simulation results show that the framework converges stably and improves the overall system reward.

(2) To improve exploration efficiency and characterize constraint trade-offs in multi-UAV collaboration, this paper designs a request-level quality-aware action mask and a Lagrange constraint mechanism. The action mask blocks clearly infeasible cooperative-UAV and MBS actions at runtime to reduce ineffective exploration. The Lagrange constraint incorporates deadline satisfaction rate (DSR) and MBS load into an interpretable soft-constraint trade-off. Simulation results show that these mechanisms regulate MBS dependence and cooperative offloading ratios, while characterizing the trade-off among DSR, energy consumption, and MBS load.

(3) To capture long-term operational characteristics neglected when terminal energy dynamics are ignored, this paper explicitly models UAV flight energy consumption, user equipment (UE) battery dynamics, and wireless power transfer (WPT). Comparative and sensitivity experiments show that, under a unified service-request association and processing procedure, the proposed framework achieves the highest service fairness, the lowest UAV-side energy consumption, and the lowest MBS load ratio while maintaining a competitive DSR. The framework further maintains a stable trade-off among energy efficiency, fairness, and service quality under varying UE loads and UAV computing capacities.
\end{abstract*}
