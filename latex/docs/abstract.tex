\begin{abstract}
随着 5G 技术的快速发展和迭代，多无人机辅助移动边缘计算（Multi-UAV MEC）系统逐渐成为未来通信网络的重要组成部分。凭借灵活的机动部署、视距链路和按需覆盖等优势，无人机辅助边缘计算在应急通信、智慧城市、低空智能网络等场景中展现出巨大的应用潜力，可有效增强边缘覆盖、任务接入和协同计算能力。然而，多无人机 MEC 系统的优化仍然面临诸多挑战，包括无人机轨迹规划、请求级卸载决策、截止期约束、宏基站（MBS）回传负载以及终端设备能量动态等问题。由于无人机的飞行特性和无线信道环境的动态变化，传统的启发式优化算法难以高效应对这些复杂问题。近年来，多智能体强化学习（Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, MARL）算法在解决复杂的协同决策问题中表现出色。因此，将 MARL 算法应用于多无人机 MEC 系统的轨迹与任务卸载协同优化研究，以提升系统服务质量、降低 UAV 侧能耗、改善用户公平性，具有重要的研究意义。

本文基于多智能体强化学习，针对多无人机 MEC 系统中轨迹控制与任务卸载的强耦合问题，设计和开发新的双层协同优化框架，并在正式实验口径中采用最近 UAV 兜底接入机制，使所有服务请求均被系统接入并尝试处理。具体研究内容与贡献如下：

（1）针对多无人机 MEC 系统中连续轨迹控制与离散卸载决策合并导致训练复杂度高的问题，本文提出了一种基于注意力机制的多智能体近端策略优化（Attention-MAPPO）框架。该框架将协同调度拆分为上层轨迹控制和下层请求级卸载两个决策层：上层采用 Attention-MAPPO 建模无人机之间的协同关系并输出轨迹动作；下层采用约束注意力卸载 MAPPO，在每个有效服务请求上选择本地无人机执行、协作无人机执行或 MBS 执行。最后通过仿真实验验证，所提双层框架具有稳定的收敛性，该方法能够有效提升系统的综合奖励。

（2）针对多 UAV 协同场景下不可行动作探索效率低和约束权衡难以刻画的问题，本文设计了请求级质量感知动作掩码和拉格朗日约束机制。质量感知动作掩码对明显不可行的协作 UAV 与 MBS 动作进行运行时屏蔽，减少无效探索；拉格朗日约束项将截止期满足率（DSR）与 MBS 负载纳入可解释的约束权衡。仿真实验表明，所提机制能够调节 MBS 依赖和协作卸载比例，并揭示 DSR、能耗与 MBS 负载之间的偏好调节边界。

（3）针对现有工作忽略终端设备能量动态导致系统长期运行不可持续的问题，本文在系统模型中显式建模了无人机飞行能耗、用户设备（UE）电池动态和无线能量传输（WPT）机制。force-admission 扩展基线实验表明，所提框架在所有服务请求均被接入并尝试处理的前提下，取得最高服务公平性、最低 UAV 侧能耗和最低 MBS 负载比例，同时保持较好的请求加权 DSR，验证了该框架在全服务接入条件下的多目标折中优势。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
With the rapid development and iteration of 5G technology, multi-UAV assisted mobile edge computing (Multi-UAV MEC) systems have gradually become an important component of future communication networks. With the advantages of flexible maneuvering deployment, line-of-sight links, and on-demand coverage, UAV-assisted edge computing has shown great application potential in scenarios such as emergency communications, smart cities, and low-altitude intelligent networks, which can effectively enhance edge coverage, task admission, and cooperative computing capabilities. However, the optimization of multi-UAV MEC systems still faces numerous challenges, including UAV trajectory planning, per-request offloading decisions, deadline constraints, macro base station (MBS) backhaul load, and terminal device energy dynamics. Due to the flight characteristics of UAVs and the dynamic changes of wireless channel environments, traditional heuristic optimization algorithms are difficult to efficiently address these complex problems. In recent years, multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) algorithms have shown excellent performance in solving complex collaborative decision-making problems. Therefore, applying MARL algorithms to the collaborative optimization of trajectory and task offloading in multi-UAV MEC systems, in order to improve system service quality, reduce UAV-side energy consumption, and enhance user fairness, has important research significance.

Based on multi-agent reinforcement learning, this paper designs and develops a novel hierarchical collaborative optimization framework for the strong coupling problem between trajectory control and task offloading in multi-UAV MEC systems. In the final evaluation setting, a nearest-UAV fallback admission rule is adopted so that every service request is admitted by the system and attempted for processing. The specific research contents and contributions are as follows:

(1) To address the problem of high training complexity caused by combining continuous trajectory control with discrete offloading decisions in multi-UAV MEC systems, this paper proposes an Attention-MAPPO framework based on the attention mechanism. The framework decomposes collaborative scheduling into an upper-layer trajectory control and a lower-layer per-request offloading decision layer: the upper layer employs Attention-MAPPO to model inter-UAV coordination and output trajectory actions; the lower layer employs constrained attention offload MAPPO to select local UAV execution, cooperative UAV execution, or MBS execution for each valid service request. Finally, simulation experiments verify that the proposed hierarchical framework has stable convergence and can effectively improve the system's comprehensive reward.

(2) To address the problems of low exploration efficiency of infeasible actions and difficult characterization of constraint trade-offs in multi-UAV collaborative scenarios, this paper designs a request-level quality-aware action mask and a Lagrange constraint mechanism. The quality-aware action mask performs runtime masking of clearly infeasible cooperative UAV and MBS actions to reduce ineffective exploration; the Lagrange constraint term incorporates deadline satisfaction rate (DSR) and MBS load into an interpretable constraint trade-off. Simulation experiments show that the proposed mechanism can regulate MBS dependence and cooperative offloading ratios, and reveal the preference-adjustment boundary among DSR, energy consumption, and MBS load.

(3) To address the problem of unsustainability in long-term system operation caused by existing works ignoring terminal device energy dynamics, this paper explicitly models UAV flight energy consumption, user equipment (UE) battery dynamics, and wireless power transfer (WPT) mechanisms in the system model. Force-admission experiments show that, when all service requests are admitted and attempted for processing, the proposed framework achieves the highest service fairness, the lowest UAV-side energy consumption, and the lowest MBS load ratio while maintaining a competitive request-weighted DSR, verifying its multi-objective trade-off advantage under full service admission.
\end{abstract*}
