\begin{abstract}
无人机（UAV）集群为动态地面需求提供时限计算服务时，需要协调空间覆盖、跨节点分工与有限传输计算资源。轨迹策略若看不到尚未接纳的需求，难以及时形成服务机会；增加接纳又可能加剧批量资源竞争与跨时隙排队。本文以临时空地计算服务为应用动机，围绕需求感知轨迹、固定快照任务分配和动态排队联合控制开展研究。以全部实际生成请求为分母定义端到端截止期满足率（DSR），并通过接纳率与接纳后完成率分解其变化。

首先，在含服务程序缓存与最终批量算力均分的自然覆盖协议下，保持 $100\,\mathrm{m}$ 物理服务半径不变，将感知范围内尚未覆盖需求纳入 Attention-MAPPO 的预动作观测。独立确认中，扩展观测候选 DSR 从0.2450增至0.2735（提升2.85个百分点，训练种子配对 $95\%$ 区间为 $[+1.65,+4.06]$ 个百分点），说明需求可见性能够改善指定主场景的接纳与服务。其次，针对跨机任务迁移通过最终批量共享算力影响其他请求的现象，提出按超时数与平均归一化时延两层字典序整体重评估的 MC-ITR 固定快照求解方法。主配置每快照平均超时数由迭代对照的2.215降至2.045；扩展观测候选轨迹的冻结快照桥接进一步显示每快照平均减少2.633个超时请求的分配质量余量，同时伴随较高搜索开销。MC-ITR 未作为后续动态排队方法的在线模块或蒸馏教师。

进一步，在独立的跨时隙动态排队协议中，研究上传、转发和先占式最早截止期优先（EDF）计算跨时隙保留进度时的轨迹与卸载联合控制。结合释放时间工作保守串联排队近似，构建候选级双分支等变策略，通过两轮数据聚合蒸馏规划知识，再利用完整环境轨迹与共同随机数下的短视界反事实辅助评分进行 PPO 微调。该辅助评分与真实轨迹奖励分别定义，不将混合更新视为标准在策略 GRPO。测试提供零在线 Engine 前瞻策略，以及学习先验同时参与候选筛选和评分的神经引导 Top-6 MPC。

三个固定父谱系、八个新工作负载与两个动作流构成816个配对确认回合。联合微调 DSR 为48.74\%，相对从零冷启动策略（44.25\%）提高4.49个百分点，相对蒸馏学生初值（48.04\%）提高0.70个百分点（31项比较 Bonferroni 校正区间为 $[+0.39,+0.98]$ 个百分点）；受控反事实开关消融表明，无反事实辅助的纯暖启动 PPO-GAE 达到48.05\%（较冷启动提高3.80个百分点），短视界反事实辅助进一步贡献0.69个百分点的净增量。在暖启动内部，双分支耦合相对真去耦微调（48.41\%）与无期望向量微调（48.63\%）的全程 DSR 差值分别为 $+0.33$ 与 $+0.11$ 个百分点（$95\%$ 区间跨零），但将池化已接纳任务条件完成率提升至92.69\%（描述性提升 $+0.90$ 与 $+0.59$ 个百分点，48回合算术均值为92.73\%）。微调 Top-6 MPC 全程 DSR 达49.31\%，相对指定在线规划基线（48.56\%）提高0.75个百分点（校正区间为 $[+0.25,+1.42]$ 个百分点）。两种控制方式平均决策耗时分别为0.520秒和0.722秒，低于基线1.602秒，但尾延迟仍超过一秒。单时隙批量共享协议与跨时隙动态排队协议的绝对指标不能直接拼接。本文据此形成从需求可见性、批量资源竞争到动态排队控制的分层证据，为进一步纳入信息成本与决策延迟的集群服务方法提供基础。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
A UAV swarm providing deadline-constrained computation services must coordinate spatial coverage, task offloading, and limited communication and computing resources. A trajectory policy that cannot observe unadmitted demand may miss service opportunities, whereas admitting more requests can increase batch contention and persistent queues. Motivated by temporary air--ground computation services, this thesis studies demand-aware trajectories, fixed-snapshot allocation, and joint control under dynamic queues. End-to-end deadline satisfaction rate (DSR) uses all actually generated requests as its denominator and is decomposed into admission and conditional completion.

First, under the natural-coverage batch-service protocol with service caching and equal batch compute sharing, uncovered demand within the sensing range is included in the pre-action observations of Attention-MAPPO while retaining the 100-m service radius. Independent confirmation increases DSR from 0.2450 to 0.2735 (+2.85 percentage points, 95\% paired $t$ CI [+1.65, +4.06]) in the specified main setting. Second, the fixed-snapshot MC-ITR method reevaluates complete assignments under a strict two-level lexicographic objective of deadline misses and mean normalized latency to account for the effects of task migration on other requests through batch-shared computing. It reduces mean deadline misses from 2.215 for an iterative comparator to 2.045. Frozen shared-trajectory snapshots from the extended-observation candidate identify further allocation headroom (-2.633 deadline misses per snapshot), accompanied by higher search cost. MC-ITR is neither an online component nor the distillation teacher of the subsequent dynamic-queue method.

An independent dynamic-queue protocol is then used to study joint trajectory and offloading control when upload, forwarding, and preemptive earliest-deadline-first (EDF) computing retain progress across slots. Coupled with a release-time work-conserving tandem-queue approximation, a two-branch candidate-equivariant policy first acquires planning knowledge through two rounds of data aggregation and distillation. Complete environment trajectories and short-horizon counterfactual scores under common random numbers then support PPO fine-tuning. Counterfactual scores are distinguished from actual trajectory rewards, and the mixed update is not interpreted as standard on-policy GRPO. Evaluation considers a policy without online Engine lookahead and a neural-guided Top-6 MPC in which the learned prior affects both candidate selection and scoring.

Confirmation comprises 816 paired evaluation episodes across three fixed parent lineages, eight new workloads, and two action streams. Joint fine-tuning achieves 48.74\% DSR, improving over cold-start training (44.25\%) by 4.49 percentage points and over its distilled student initialization (48.04\%) by 0.70 percentage points (interval corrected across 31 comparisons: [+0.39, +0.98] percentage points). Controlled ablation of the counterfactual auxiliary term shows that pure warm-start PPO-GAE without counterfactual rollouts reaches 48.05\% (+3.80 percentage points over cold start), while short-horizon counterfactual assistance contributes the remaining +0.69 percentage points. Within warm-start fine-tuning, overall DSR differences against truly decomposed fine-tuning (48.41\%) and expectation-ablated fine-tuning (48.63\%) are +0.33 and +0.11 percentage points (with 95\% CIs crossing zero), accompanied by descriptive improvements of +0.90 and +0.59 percentage points in pooled conditional completion rate among admitted tasks (reaching 92.69\%, with a 48-episode mean of 92.73\%). Fine-tuned Top-6 MPC achieves 49.31\% DSR, exceeding the specified online planner (48.56\%) by 0.75 percentage points (corrected interval: [+0.25, +1.42]). Mean decision times are 0.520 and 0.722 seconds, compared with 1.602 seconds for the planner, although tail latencies still exceed one second. Because the single-slot batch protocol and the multi-slot dynamic-queue protocol use distinct execution semantics, their absolute results cannot be pooled across chapters. These findings provide bounded evidence connecting demand visibility, batch contention, and dynamic-queue control, and a basis for studying reporting costs and decision delays.
\end{abstract*}
