\begin{abstract}
在应急救援与临时通信保障等动态空地服务场景中，无人机（UAV）集群作为空中移动边缘计算（MEC）节点，需协同规划空间机动轨迹与机载计算资源分工，以保障地面用户设备（UE）动态请求的按时完成。然而，受限于单机物理覆盖半径与机载计算、缓存容量，传统方法面临未覆盖需求感知缺失、跨机任务迁移引发批量算力竞争、以及在线调度算法自身耗时挤占任务截止期余量等严峻挑战。为此，本文面向自然覆盖接纳约束下的无人机集群时限计算服务，以全部实际生成请求为基准定义端到端截止期满足率（DSR = 接纳率 $\times$ 接纳后按时完成率），围绕“集群飞向哪里、任务交给谁执行、如何实现低开销在线闭环协同”三个核心问题开展深入研究。本文的主要工作与创新成果如下：

针对自然覆盖约束下未接纳需求不可见导致的空间部署盲区问题，提出了一种基于预接纳观测扩展的需求感知协同轨迹规划方法。基于集中式训练与分散式执行（CTDE）架构构建上层多智能体近端策略优化（Attention-MAPPO）网络，设计确定性距离排序的预接纳需求槽位填充机制，在不改变 $100\text{ m}$ 物理服务半径的前提下，将 $460\text{ m}$ 感知范围内的待接纳用户请求特征纳入局部观测；进而利用多头交叉注意力机制动态聚合邻机几何拓扑与地面需求分布，结合运动可行性修复输出协同机动指令。在 100 UE 热点仿真主场景及理想需求信息假设下，相同注意力架构的观测对照显示，接纳率由 $49.96\%$ 增至 $61.89\%$，端到端 DSR 由 $24.44\%$ 提升至 $27.93\%$，而接纳后按时完成率由 $49.19\%$ 降至 $45.37\%$；机队平均能耗降低 $6.37\%$，Jain 公平性指数增加 $0.0786$。结果支持需求可见性改善服务接纳，尚不能分离注意力结构的独立贡献或确定条件完成率下降的原因。

针对批量算力均分引发的跨机迁移外部性，提出基于全批次重评估的截止期优先计算任务分配方法（MC-ITR）。分析单请求迁移对其他请求时延及截止期状态的影响，构建以超时请求数、平均归一化时延和分配能耗为优先级的严格字典序目标，在单请求迁移邻域内搜索系统级改善，并给出严格下降、有限终止及无改善退出时的邻域局部最优性质。固定快照主实验中，相对共享初值与邻域的个体评价迭代方法，MC-ITR 将每快照平均超时数从 $2.228$ 降至 $2.055$，减少约 $7.8\%$，同时模型分配能耗与记录搜索耗时分别增加约 $13.7\%$ 和 $30.7\%$。历史需求感知轨迹上的反事实分配平均减少 $2.46$ 个超时请求。这些结果支持既定仿真模型内的离线分配质量改善，不构成全局最优或在线闭环收益保证。

针对组合优化搜索耗时难以满足高动态在线时限要求的问题，提出了一种计及决策开销的无人机集群分层服务决策框架。上层采用需求感知 Attention-MAPPO 策略生成协同机动动作；下层将高维离散卸载问题解耦为“节点级整数配额提议”与“请求级容量槽线性指派”，在仅需至多 $10$ 次条件匹配的有限计算预算内高效压缩搜索空间。同时，设计了显式区分缓存命中与未命中分支的解析缓存混合评分函数，结合候选间能耗定价机制实现服务质量与资源消耗的动态权衡，并将单步完整软件求解耗时实时注入任务时延反馈与状态闭环演化。在 $5$ 架无人机服务 $100$ 个移动用户的闭环仿真中，所提分层方法的单次完整决策耗时均值仅为 $17.72\text{ ms}$（$95\%$ 分位数为 $24.69\text{ ms}$），计入真实求解延迟后的端到端 DSR 达到 $33.76\%$，在满足机队能耗约束的前提下，较共同贪心修复基线和同框架二值评分策略分别显著提升 $1.03$ 和 $0.26$ 个百分点，验证了所提框架在在线时限服务中的高效性与鲁棒性。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
In dynamic air-ground service scenarios such as emergency rescue and temporary communications, an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarm acting as aerial mobile edge computing (MEC) nodes must coordinate spatial trajectory planning with onboard computation resource allocation to guarantee the timely completion of deadline-constrained user equipment (UE) requests. However, constrained by limited physical coverage radii as well as finite onboard computation and caching capacities, conventional approaches face critical challenges: blindness to uncovered spatial demand, batch computation contention induced by cross-UAV task migration, and online scheduling latency that erodes request deadline margins. To address these challenges under natural coverage-based admission, this thesis defines the end-to-end deadline satisfaction rate ($\text{DSR} = \text{admission rate} \times \text{conditional completion rate}$) over all generated service requests, and systematically investigates three core problems: where the swarm should move, how admitted tasks should be allocated across execution nodes, and how to integrate movement with low-overhead online allocation. The main contributions of this thesis are summarized as follows:

To resolve spatial deployment blindness caused by the invisibility of uncovered demand under natural coverage constraints, a demand-aware cooperative trajectory planning method based on pre-admission observation expansion is proposed. Built upon the centralized training with decentralized execution (CTDE) paradigm and an attention-based multi-agent proximal policy optimization (Attention-MAPPO) network, a deterministic distance-ordered slot-filling mechanism is designed to incorporate uncovered UE requests within a $460\text{ m}$ sensing range into the local observation while retaining the $100\text{ m}$ physical service radius. Multi-head cross-attention is utilized to dynamically aggregate neighbor UAV topologies and ground demand distributions, generating feasible cooperative movement actions. In the 100-UE hotspot simulation with ideal demand information, an observation comparison using the same attention architecture increases admission from $49.96\%$ to $61.89\%$ and end-to-end DSR from $24.44\%$ to $27.93\%$, while conditional deadline satisfaction decreases from $49.19\%$ to $45.37\%$. Average fleet energy decreases by $6.37\%$ and Jain's fairness index increases by $0.0786$. These results support the value of demand visibility for admission, but do not isolate an independent attention-architecture benefit or identify the cause of the conditional-quality decrease.

To address migration externalities under batch computation sharing, a deadline-prioritized Multi-Candidate Iterative Task Reallocation (MC-ITR) method based on full-batch reevaluation is developed. Single-request migration is analyzed in terms of its effects on other requests' latencies and deadline outcomes. The method searches single-request neighborhoods using a strict lexicographic objective prioritizing deadline misses, normalized latency, and assignment energy, with strict descent, finite termination, and neighborhood local optimality upon non-improving termination. In the main fixed-snapshot experiment, MC-ITR reduces mean deadline misses from $2.228$ to $2.055$ relative to an own-request iterative baseline sharing its initialization and neighborhood, an improvement of approximately $7.8\%$, while model assignment energy and recorded search time increase by approximately $13.7\%$ and $30.7\%$, respectively. Counterfactual allocation along historical demand-aware trajectories reduces deadline misses by $2.46$ per snapshot. These results support offline allocation improvements within the specified simulation model, without guarantees of global optimality or online closed-loop gains.

To bridge the gap between combinatorial allocation quality and strict online decision-time budgets, a cost-aware hierarchical service decision framework is proposed. Combining the demand-aware Attention-MAPPO trajectory policy in the upper layer with a structured lower-layer scheduler, the high-dimensional offloading problem is decomposed into node-level integer quota proposals and request-level capacity-slot linear assignments within a strict budget of at most $10$ matching calls. Furthermore, an analytical cache-mixture scoring function is formulated to decouple cache-hit and cache-miss deadline branches, integrated with candidate-level energy pricing and closed-loop injection of actual software execution latency. In closed-loop evaluations with $5$ UAVs serving $100$ UEs, the hierarchical method achieves a mean full-call decision latency of only $17.72\text{ ms}$ ($\text{p95} = 24.69\text{ ms}$) and attains a cost-aware end-to-end DSR of $33.76\%$, outperforming the greedy-repair baseline and the binary-scoring priced strategy by $1.03$ and $0.26$ percentage points, respectively, within prespecified fleet energy constraints.
\end{abstract*}
