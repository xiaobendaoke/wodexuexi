\begin{abstract}
无人机（UAV）集群为动态地面请求提供时限计算服务时，需要协调空中平台的空间部署与机载资源分工。单机覆盖与计算、缓存能力有限，协同机动增加的接纳需求又可能加剧执行节点的资源竞争。本文以应急场景中的临时空地服务为应用动机，采用动态请求驱动的逐决策步批次移动边缘计算模型，研究“集群飞向哪里、任务交给谁执行、如何连接机动与分配”三个任务规划问题。自然覆盖条件下，请求须先进入物理服务范围，再经过计算任务分配按时完成。本文以全部实际生成服务请求为分母定义端到端截止期满足率（DSR），并报告接纳率、接纳后质量、UAV 能耗、公平性、宏基站（MBS）依赖与计算开销。

首先，在保持 $100\,\mathrm{m}$ 物理服务半径不变的条件下，将感知范围内未覆盖需求纳入上层 Attention-MAPPO 的预动作观测，结合邻机与用户信息形成需求感知轨迹决策。独立确认中，DSR 从 $0.2444$ 提高至 $0.2793$，接纳扩展同时伴随条件完成率下降。其次，针对跨机迁移通过批量算力共享影响其他请求的现象，提出固定快照下整体重评估的 MC-ITR 分配方法。主配置每快照平均超时数从迭代对照的 $2.228$ 降至 $2.055$；需求感知上层与无注意力学习式下层产生的轨迹快照也显示分配余量，但伴随更高能耗与求解时间。MC-ITR 据此用于离线分析，在线系统采用有限预算的配额匹配。

在此基础上，构建 SF-HLM 的分层协同决策：冻结需求感知轨迹上层，独立学习下层配额搜索并结合条件化负载匹配。对自然接纳请求构建请求--节点二部图，固定节点配额后通过容量槽指派生成完整分配，CompleteQuotaPPO 在有限匹配预算内选择配额迁移与停止动作，并结合共同质量掩码、普通修复、能耗筛选和最好方案保留。上层按各 UAV 的观测分散推理，下层集中调度当前请求批次，保持原批次评价时序与奖励。五架 UAV、一百个地面用户设备（UE）主场景的十个独立训练块与五个配对工作负载确认表明，SF-HLM 的 DSR 为 $34.244\%$，相对贪心修复、冻结请求级下层和适配请求级下层修复，分别提高 $1.423$、$2.008$ 和 $2.192$ 个百分点。相对相同学习上层加逐请求随机卸载及全随机，分别提高 $10.604$ 和 $18.816$ 个百分点，支持完整下层优化包与完整系统的服务收益。

共享强框架下的配额随机搜索 DSR 为约 $34.100\%$，学习搜索额外增量约为 $0.143$ 个百分点，其统计敏感性与较高软件开销需要同时解释。SF-HLM 各块隔离 p95 软件调用时间的均值约为 $109.24\,\mathrm{ms}$。冻结策略的 UE 数量与 UAV 算力九格评价、固定潜在请求分母复核以及快照预算诊断进一步检验了结果边界。主 DSR 未计调度等待；原确认轨迹事后加入逐次隔离软件费用后，学习、强随机与贪心的 DSR 分别为 $29.379\%$、$30.073\%$ 和 $32.161\%$，排序发生改变。计算能耗采用未标定具体芯片的等效频率模型。现有证据支持规定批次评分与简化二维运动下的任务规划比较，实际在线净收益仍需结合调度费用及资源占用检验。本文为计入运动执行、信息上报与协调成本的后续研究提供了可复核依据。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
An unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarm providing deadline-constrained computation services must coordinate spatial deployment with the use of onboard resources. Individual coverage, computation, and caching capacity are limited, while admitting more demand through cooperative movement can increase execution-node contention. Motivated by temporary air--ground services in emergency settings, this thesis uses a dynamic-request, decision-step batch model of mobile edge computing to study three task-planning questions: where the swarm should move, which nodes should execute the tasks, and how to connect movement with allocation. Under natural coverage-based admission, requests must enter the physical service area before they can be allocated for timely completion. The end-to-end deadline satisfaction rate (DSR) uses all actually generated service requests as its denominator. Admission, conditional completion, UAV energy, fairness, macro base station (MBS) dependence, and computation cost are also reported.

First, uncovered demand within the sensing range is added to the pre-action observations of an Attention-MAPPO upper layer while retaining the 100-m physical service radius. Neighbor and user features support demand-aware trajectory decisions. Independent confirmation increases DSR from 0.2444 to 0.2793, with higher admission accompanied by lower conditional completion. Second, the fixed-snapshot MC-ITR method reevaluates complete assignments to account for the effects of task migration on other requests through batch-shared computation. It reduces the main-setting mean deadline misses per snapshot from 2.228 for an iterative comparator to 2.055. Snapshots from a demand-aware upper policy with a non-attention learned lower layer also reveal assignment-quality headroom, accompanied by higher energy and computation costs. MC-ITR provides an offline allocation reference; the online system uses quota matching within a finite budget.

The resulting SF-HLM hierarchy combines a frozen demand-aware trajectory upper layer with independently learned quota search and conditional load matching in the lower layer. Admitted requests and execution nodes form a bipartite graph. For a fixed node-quota vector, capacity-slot assignment produces a complete placement; CompleteQuotaPPO selects quota transfers and a stop action within a finite matching budget. A common quality mask, ordinary repair, energy screening, and best-solution retention support execution. Upper-layer inference is decentralized according to each UAV's observations, whereas the lower layer centrally schedules the current request batch, preserving the original batch-evaluation timing and reward. In the main setting with five UAVs and 100 user devices (UEs), confirmation over ten independent training blocks and five paired workloads yields a DSR of 34.244\%. Gains over greedy repair, a frozen request-level lower layer with repair, and its adapted counterpart are 1.423, 2.008, and 2.192 percentage points, respectively. Gains over per-request random offloading with the same learned upper layer and over fully random control are 10.604 and 18.816 percentage points, supporting the benefits of the complete lower-layer optimization package and the complete system.

Quota-random search within the same strong framework already reaches approximately 34.100\% DSR. The additional learned-search gain is approximately 0.143 percentage points and requires interpretation alongside its statistical sensitivity and higher software cost. The mean of the block-level isolated p95 software-call times is approximately 109.24 ms. Nine frozen-policy UE-count and UAV-compute settings, a common potential-request denominator check, and snapshot budget diagnostics further assess the scope of the results. The primary DSR excludes scheduling wait. Post-hoc scoring of the original trajectories with recorded per-call isolated software costs yields DSRs of 29.379\%, 30.073\%, and 32.161\% for learned search, strong random search, and greedy repair, respectively, changing their ordering. Computation energy follows an equivalent-frequency model without chip-specific calibration. The evidence supports task-planning comparisons under the specified batch scoring and planar-motion model; net online benefits require joint assessment of scheduling costs and resource occupancy. The findings provide a reproducible basis for further assessment of motion execution, reporting, and coordination costs.
\end{abstract*}
