\begin{abstract}
随着低空经济与智能应急响应的快速发展，无人机集群辅助移动边缘计算已成为向地面动态终端提供低时延计算服务的重要形态。在实际空地协同服务过程中，受限于机载有限物理覆盖半径、异构服务缓存与计算容量以及多节点资源竞争，无人机轨迹机动、前端任务接纳与多节点卸载分配之间呈现出强烈的时空耦合特征。围绕受限自然覆盖与多节点资源竞争下如何提升系统端到端截止期满足率这一核心问题，本文对空地协同计算系统统一建模、需求感知协同轨迹规划、二元字典序任务分配以及跨时隙持续排队协同控制展开系统研究。主要工作如下：

针对空地协同计算系统中空间几何关联、多链路无线传输与异构节点计算负载紧密耦合的问题，建立了面向时限计算服务的无人机集群空地协同计算统一数学模型与受控双协议评价体系。首先，在高低层控制时间尺度分离框架下，构建了最近邻自然覆盖终端关联准则、离散机动防碰安全间距修复模型以及频段隔离视距无线通信模型，严格证明了最小安全间距约束下自然覆盖关联集合的互斥完备划分性质。随后，统一刻画了接入无人机本地计算、邻机协同中继与地面宏基站回传三类执行路径，分别建立了单时隙批量等份算力共享与跨时隙先占式最早截止期优先串联排队两类互补负载模型，并导出且证明了端到端截止期满足率双因子精确分解与同源工作负载配对差分定理。该统一模型清晰解耦了前端空间接纳能力与后端资源执行质量，为后续协同规划与控制方法设计奠定了理论基础。

基于单时隙自然覆盖与批量算力共享模型，考虑分层强化学习中覆盖圈外终端不可见引发的上层策略观测混叠问题，提出了一种融合感知范围未覆盖需求扩展观测与精简下层协同的分层多智能体强化学习轨迹规划方法。首先，从策略梯度可观测性角度证明了原始仅含已关联终端的局部观测对圈外需求分布的梯度盲区性质，并通过瓶颈诊断明确了未覆盖丢弃对时限服务失败的主导制约作用。随后，在不扩大物理覆盖半径且不增加固定观测张量维度的前提下，按距离升序将感知范围内未覆盖活跃终端填入上层关系自注意力网络的空闲槽位，并配合在基准对比中更为稳健的下层无注意力精简卸载策略开展协同训练。最后，通过多组独立训练种子与盲测工作负载的配对仿真实验表明，该方法能够有效消除预接纳空间感知盲区，显著提升系统端到端截止期满足率与用户间服务公平性。

考虑到单时隙批量等份算力共享机制下单个请求跨机迁移会改变源节点与目标节点同批其余任务的完成时延，提出了一种基于全批次真实重评估的多轮协同迭代任务分配算法。首先，构建了以超时请求总数最小化为第一优先级、平均归一化时延最小化为第二优先级的严格二元字典序优化目标，克服了传统线性加权目标将异质指标折合的局限。随后，精确推导了单请求跨机迁移对源节点算力释放与目标节点算力摊薄的时延外部性增量分解，严格证明了全批次真实重评估准则下算法的单步严格字典序下降性、有限步终止性与单请求邻域局部最优性。最后，通过标准固定快照、小规模穷举校验快照及冻结轨迹桥接快照的配对对比仿真，验证了显式计入同批算力外部性对消解资源竞争与削减超时请求的有效性。

针对突发热点需求到达、已接纳关联不可变与历史队列积压持久耦合下的跨时隙协同控制问题，设计了一种融合两轮规划知识蒸馏、共同随机数反事实辅助微调与先验裁剪模型预测控制的联合控制框架。首先，建立了单通道通信释放时刻递推结合节点工作守恒先占式最早截止期优先推进的两阶段串联排队近似模型，构建了引入上层期望运动反馈的候选槽位置换同变双分支策略网络，并证明了槽位置换同变性、同源随机流反事实组中心化的外生扰动消去性质及幂次先验校准的严格保序性。随后，采用两轮在线状态聚合将离线多步规划教师偏好蒸馏至策略网络，结合三步反事实辅助优势项开展近端策略优化微调以实现零前瞻快速推理，并利用先验裁剪构建神经引导滚动规划方案。最后，通过多场景盲测配对数值仿真验证了所提框架在提升截止期满足率与大幅缩减在线决策计算耗时方面的优越性。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
With the rapid development of the low-altitude economy and emergency response, unmanned aerial vehicle swarm-assisted mobile edge computing has become a vital paradigm for providing low-latency computation services to ground terminals. Constrained by limited physical coverage radius, heterogeneous computing capacities, and multi-node resource contention, trajectory movement, task admission, and offloading allocation exhibit strong spatiotemporal coupling. Focusing on enhancing end-to-end deadline satisfaction under these constraints, this thesis investigates unified system modeling, demand-aware trajectory planning, binary lexicographic task allocation, and cross-slot queuing control. The main work is as follows:

Aiming at the tight coupling among spatial association, wireless transmission, and heterogeneous node workloads, a unified mathematical model and a controlled dual-protocol evaluation framework for deadline-constrained services are established. First, under control timescale separation, nearest-neighbor natural-coverage association, collision-avoidance maneuver repair, and frequency-isolated line-of-sight channel models are formulated, and the mutually exclusive partition property of association sets under minimum safety spacing is proved. Subsequently, local, neighbor-relay, and macro base station backhaul execution paths are modeled under single-slot batch equal-share computing and cross-slot preemptive earliest-deadline-first tandem queuing, and exact two-factor deadline satisfaction decomposition and common-workload paired difference theorems are derived.

Based on the single-slot natural-coverage and batch-shared computing model, considering upper-level observation aliasing caused by invisible uncovered terminals, a demand-aware hierarchical multi-agent reinforcement learning trajectory planning method is proposed. First, the policy-gradient blindness of associated-only local observations to out-of-coverage demand is proved, and bottleneck diagnostics confirm that uncovered drops dominate service failures. Subsequently, without expanding the physical coverage radius or fixed observation dimensions, uncovered active terminals within the sensing range are sorted by distance into vacant slots of the upper-level relational self-attention network and trained jointly with a streamlined attention-free lower-level offloading policy. Finally, paired simulations verify significant improvements in deadline satisfaction rate and service fairness.

Considering that single-request cross-node migration under batch equal-share computing alters completion latencies of co-scheduled tasks on both source and destination nodes, a multi-round cooperative iterative task allocation algorithm based on full-batch exact re-evaluation is proposed. First, a strict binary lexicographic objective prioritizing total deadline misses followed by mean normalized latency is constructed. Second, exact latency externality increments of single-request migration are derived, and strict lexicographic descent, finite-step termination, and single-request neighborhood local optimality are proved. Finally, paired simulations on standard, exhaustive benchmark, and frozen bridge snapshots verify its effectiveness in mitigating batch externalities and reducing deadline misses.

To address cross-slot joint control under bursty arrivals, immutable post-admission association, and persistent queue backlogs, a control framework integrating two-round planning distillation, common random number counterfactual-assisted fine-tuning, and prior-pruned model predictive control is designed. First, a two-stage tandem queue approximation coupling communication release-time recursion with preemptive earliest-deadline-first computing is formulated alongside a slot-permutation-equivariant dual-branch policy network, with proofs of permutation equivariance, disturbance cancellation via counterfactual group centering, and order preservation under power-law calibration. Subsequently, two-round online state aggregation distillation is combined with counterfactual-assisted proximal policy optimization fine-tuning for zero-lookahead inference and prior-pruned receding-horizon planning. Finally, multi-scenario paired simulations confirm improved deadline satisfaction and substantially reduced online decision latency.
\end{abstract*}
