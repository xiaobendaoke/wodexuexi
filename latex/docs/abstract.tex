\begin{abstract}
无人机（UAV）集群为动态地面请求提供时限计算服务时，需要协调空中平台的空间部署与机载资源分工。单机覆盖与计算、缓存能力有限，协同机动增加的接纳需求又可能加剧执行节点的资源竞争。本文以应急场景中的临时空地服务为应用动机，采用动态请求驱动的移动边缘计算模型，研究“集群飞向哪里、任务交给谁执行、如何连接机动与分配”三个任务规划问题。自然覆盖条件下，请求须先进入物理服务范围，再经过计算任务分配按时完成。本文以全部实际生成服务请求为分母定义端到端截止期满足率（DSR），并报告接纳率、接纳后质量、UAV 能耗、公平性、宏基站（MBS）依赖与计算开销。

首先，建立自然覆盖仿真与分层决策模型，在保持 $100\,\mathrm{m}$ 物理服务半径不变的条件下，研究上层 Attention-MAPPO 对感知范围内尚未覆盖需求的预动作观测，结合邻机与用户信息形成需求感知的协同轨迹决策。独立确认中，扩展观测候选的 DSR 从 $0.2444$ 增至 $0.2793$，增加接纳的同时揭示接纳后资源竞争。其次，在固定轨迹与历史状态下，针对跨机任务迁移通过最终批量算力共享影响其他请求的现象，研究整体重评估的 MC-ITR 计算任务分配。主配置每快照平均超时数由迭代对照的 $2.228$ 降至 $2.055$；历史候选轨迹上的桥接进一步显示质量余量，但其分配能耗和完整调用时间较高，故作为离线诊断，未直接接入在线系统。

在此基础上，构建 SF-HLM 的分层协同决策：冻结需求感知轨迹上层，独立学习下层配额搜索并结合条件化负载匹配。对自然接纳请求构建请求--节点二部图，固定节点配额后通过容量槽指派生成完整分配，CompleteQuotaPPO 在有限匹配预算内选择配额迁移与停止动作，并结合共同质量掩码、普通修复、能耗筛选和最好方案保留。上层按各 UAV 的观测分散推理，下层集中调度当前请求批次，保持原环境时序与奖励。五架 UAV、一百个地面用户设备（UE）主场景的十个独立训练块与五个配对工作负载确认表明，主线 DSR 为 $34.244\%$，相对贪心修复、原冻结和原适配学习下层修复，分别提高 $1.423$、$2.008$ 和 $2.192$ 个百分点。相对相同学习上层加逐请求随机卸载及全随机，分别提高 $10.604$ 和 $18.816$ 个百分点，支持完整下层优化包与完整系统的服务收益。

共享强框架下的配额随机搜索 DSR 为约 $34.100\%$，学习搜索额外增量约为 $0.143$ 个百分点，其统计敏感性与较高软件开销需要同时解释。主线各块隔离 p95 软件调用时间的均值约为 $109.24\,\mathrm{ms}$。冻结策略的 UE 数量与 UAV 算力九格评价、固定潜在请求分母复核以及快照预算诊断进一步检验了结果边界。现有证据支持简化二维运动及既定信息条件下的集群任务层服务规划优势，未建立注意力结构的独立最优性或实际飞行部署效果。本文为进一步计入运动执行、信息上报与协调成本的无人机集群任务规划提供了可复核的模型和方法依据。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
An unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarm providing deadline-constrained computation services must coordinate spatial deployment with the use of onboard resources. Individual coverage, computation, and caching capacity are limited, while admitting more demand through cooperative movement can increase execution-node contention. Motivated by temporary air--ground services in emergency settings, this thesis uses a dynamic-request mobile edge computing model to study three task-planning questions: where the swarm should move, which nodes should execute the tasks, and how to connect movement with allocation. Under natural coverage-based admission, requests must enter the physical service area before they can be allocated for timely completion. The end-to-end deadline satisfaction rate (DSR) uses all actually generated service requests as its denominator. Admission, conditional completion, UAV energy, fairness, macro base station (MBS) dependence, and computation cost are also reported.

First, a hierarchical simulation model is established to study pre-action observation of uncovered demand with an Attention-MAPPO upper layer, without changing the 100-m physical service radius. Neighbor and user information support demand-aware cooperative trajectory decisions. Independent confirmation increases DSR from 0.2444 to 0.2793, while revealing the resource pressure associated with increased admission. Second, the fixed-snapshot MC-ITR solver reevaluates the complete assignment after a candidate migration to account for the effects of inter-UAV task transfers on other requests through batch-shared computation. It reduces the main-setting mean deadline misses per snapshot from 2.228 for an iterative comparator to 2.055. A bridge on snapshots from a historical candidate policy identifies further assignment-quality headroom, accompanied by higher assignment energy and full-path computation time. MC-ITR therefore remains an offline diagnostic and is not directly integrated into the online system.

The resulting SF-HLM hierarchy combines a frozen demand-aware trajectory upper layer with independently learned quota search and conditional load matching in the lower layer. Admitted requests and execution nodes form a bipartite graph. For a fixed node-quota vector, capacity-slot assignment produces a complete placement; CompleteQuotaPPO selects quota transfers and a stop action within a finite matching budget. A common quality mask, ordinary repair, energy screening, and best-solution retention support execution. Upper-layer inference is decentralized according to each UAV's observations, whereas the lower layer centrally schedules the current request batch, preserving the original environment timing and reward. In the main setting with five UAVs and 100 user devices (UEs), confirmation over ten independent training blocks and five paired workloads yields a DSR of 34.244\%. Gains over greedy repair, the frozen original learned lower layer with repair, and its adapted counterpart are 1.423, 2.008, and 2.192 percentage points, respectively. Gains over per-request random offloading with the same learned upper layer and over fully random control are 10.604 and 18.816 percentage points, supporting the benefits of the complete lower-layer optimization package and the complete system.

Quota-random search within the same strong framework already reaches approximately 34.100\% DSR. The additional learned-search gain is approximately 0.143 percentage points and requires interpretation alongside its statistical sensitivity and higher software cost. The mean of the block-level isolated p95 software-call times is approximately 109.24 ms. Nine frozen-policy UE-count and UAV-compute settings, a common potential-request denominator check, and snapshot budget diagnostics further assess the scope of the results. The evidence supports a swarm task-level service-planning advantage under the simplified planar-motion model and evaluated information conditions. It does not establish isolated attention-architecture optimality or effectiveness in flight deployment. The findings provide a reproducible model and methodological basis for UAV swarm task planning that further accounts for motion execution, reporting, and coordination costs.
\end{abstract*}
